1U CubeSat Mission Hunan Launch

Slippers2Sat (S2S) 1U CubeSat

Nepal's first middle-school nano-satellite mission funded by ARDC and successfully launched into Low Earth Orbit from Hunan, China. Mentored indigenous students from Chepang, Tamang, and Dalit communities through structural assembly, harnessing, and flight readiness reviews at the Navodaya Space System Laboratory (SSL).

1U Structure ARDC Grant Navodaya SSL Amateur Satellite

Seismo-Magnetometry EPDM Lead

Earthquake Precursor Detection (EPDM)

Non-boom CubeSat payload engineered to detect Ultra-Low Frequency (<3Hz) and Extremely Low Frequency (3Hz–3kHz) seismo-electromagnetic precursors in Earth's magnetic field. Developed an array of four PNI RM3100 magnetometers with a Micron MT25QL 1Gb NOR Flash on STM32, utilizing the UBSS algorithm to isolate true ambient field signals.

4x PNI RM3100 Micron MT25QL 1Gb UBSS Algorithm IGRF Model

VLSI Design SkyWater 130nm

RISC-V 5-Stage Pipelined Processor

Synthesizable 32-bit RV32I processor core authored in Verilog HDL. Built both single-cycle and 5-stage pipelined models featuring hazard forwarding units, branch prediction, and stall logic. Integrated UART, SPI, and GPIO interfaces, verified with ModelSim, Verilator, and cocotb testbenches for Tiny Tapeout on SkyWater 130nm.

Core Technical Focus

Hardware Architecture & Firmware

STM32 Dual-Core IPCC

Asymmetric multiprocessing on Cortex-M7 (480MHz DSP/ADCS) and Cortex-M4 (240MHz Comms/LittleFS) using zero-copy SRAM3 mailboxes, hardware semaphores (HSEM), and L1 cache cleaning/invalidation.

STM32H7 / WB55

Lock-Free Ring Buffer

Single-producer single-consumer circular FIFO utilizing power-of-two masking and ARM CMSIS __DMB() data memory barriers for non-blocking UART/DMA telemetry piping without disabling interrupts.

Lock-Free FIFO

Satellite RF Modems

GMSK ($BT=0.5$), GFSK, and CW Morse beacon modulators. Assembled Layer-2 AX.25 UI frames with HDLC bit-stuffing and applied G3RUH ($1+x^{12}+x^{17}$) scrambler whitening for amateur satellite downlinks.

AX.25 & G3RUH

LittleFS Storage

Fail-safe filesystem integration on SPI NOR Flash (W25Q64/W25Q128) using copy-on-write metadata pairs and wear-leveling to protect flight telemetry during unexpected solar eclipse power loss.

Power-Loss Resilient
