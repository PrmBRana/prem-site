● SATELLITE RESEARCH FELLOW // ANTARIKCHYA PRATISTHAN NEPAL

Prem Bahadur Rana

Electronics & Communication Engineer | EPDM Mission Lead

I am a satellite research fellow and hardware engineer at Antarikchya Pratisthan Nepal (APN), focusing on nano-satellite avionics, space geomagnetism, and custom silicon processor design. I served as the EPDM Mission Lead for Nepal’s Slippers2Sat (S2S) 1U CubeSat—developed by APN with ARDC support and launched into Low Earth Orbit from Hunan, China. My engineering work spans dual-core asymmetric multiprocessing (STM32 IPCC & uORB), digital space modems (FSK, GMSK, CW, AX.25/G3RUH), fail-safe storage (LittleFS), 5-stage pipelined RISC-V design in Verilog HDL, and multilayer PCB layout (2, 4, and 6 layers). To support flight assembly, I designed the low-cost S2S satellite cleanroom equipped with Fan Filter Units (FFU) at the Navodaya Space Systems Laboratory (SSL).

Research Specializations

Core investigative domains spanning space-borne magnetometry, custom silicon microarchitecture, and robust embedded systems.

01 // GEOMAGNETISM

Space Seismo-Electromagnetics

Detecting Ultra-Low Frequency (<3Hz) and Extremely Low Frequency (3Hz–3kHz) seismo-magnetic anomalies prior to major earthquakes using compact, non-boom CubeSat sensor arrays (PNI RM3100, MMC5983MA) combined with Undetermined Blind Source Separation (UBSS).

02 // SILICON VLSI

RISC-V Silicon & FPGA Design

Designing synthesizable RV32I 5-stage pipelined processor cores in Verilog HDL with hazard forwarding units and branch prediction, targeting open-source ASIC synthesis on the SkyWater 130nm node via Tiny Tapeout, and prototyping on FPGA platforms.

03 // AVIONICS COMMS

Space Telemetry & RF Modems

Engineering space-borne amateur packet radio digipeaters (AX.25 Layer-2 UI frames), GMSK ($BT=0.5$), FSK, and CW Morse signal modulators with G3RUH ($1+x^{12}+x^{17}$) polynomial scrambling for emergency disaster communication in mountainous terrain.

04 // EMBEDDED AI

Edge AI & Agricultural Robotics

Benchmarking state-of-the-art vision architectures (YOLOv12S, YOLOv8, DETR, Faster R-CNN) on 11,675 agricultural images, deployed on low-power Raspberry Pi 4 and OpenMV MicroPython platforms for real-time automated conveyor sorting.

Seismo-Magnetometry EPDM Lead

Earthquake Precursor Detection Mission

Non-boom CubeSat payload engineered to detect Ultra-Low Frequency (<3Hz) and Extremely Low Frequency (3Hz–3kHz) seismo-magnetic anomalies in Earth's magnetic field prior to seismic events. Engineered a Quad-Mag sensor array (4x PNI RM3100, MMC5983MA) with Micron MT25QL 1Gb NOR Flash on STM32F103, utilizing the Undetermined Blind Source Separation (UBSS) algorithm to eliminate internal spacecraft magnetic noise.

4x PNI RM3100 MMC5983MA Micron MT25QL 1Gb UBSS Algorithm STM32F103
Flight Code (GitHub)
VLSI Design SkyWater 130nm

RISC-V 5-Stage Pipelined Processor Core

Designed and simulated a custom 32-bit RISC-V CPU adhering to the RV32I user-level ISA using synthesizable Verilog HDL. Implemented single-cycle and 5-stage pipelined models featuring hazard forwarding units, branch prediction, and stall logic. Integrated UART, SPI, and GPIO interfaces, verified with ModelSim, Verilator, and cocotb testbenches for Tiny Tapeout on SkyWater 130nm.

RV32I ISA Verilog HDL OpenLane Cocotb FPGA / ASIC
