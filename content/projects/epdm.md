**Nepal's First Middle School Nano-Satellite Project — Earthquake Precursor Detection Mission (EPDM)** 

The **Earthquake Precursor Detection Mission (EPDM)** is designed to use a **Quad-Mag technology payload**, consisting of four **PNI RM3100 magnetometers**, to detect and analyze electromagnetic signals in the **Ultra-Low Frequency (ULF: below 3 Hz)** and **Extremely Low Frequency (ELF: 3 Hz to 3 kHz)** ranges, which are generated from the Earth's surface before an earthquake.

Unlike traditional satellites that rely on long telescopic or deployable booms to protect magnetometers from stray magnetic fields caused by internal electric subsystems, the **EPDM** employs **Quad-Mag technology** and the **Undetermined Blind Source Separation (UBSS)** algorithm — both tested at the **University of Michigan lab** — to remove interference and accurately measure the Earth’s magnetic field.

The **PNI RM3100 magnetometers**, proven suitable for CubeSat missions, operate with high efficiency and can function reliably in space environments.  

The primary objective of the mission is to **demonstrate the capability of space-based technologies to separate stray magnetic fields from ambient magnetic fields for detecting earthquake precursor signals without using any mechanical boom.**

The Earthquake Precursor Detection Mission (EPDM) aims to **monitor Earth's magnetic field anomalies** by detecting **Ultra-Low Frequency (ULF)** and **Extremely Low Frequency (ELF)** signals that precede earthquakes, utilizing **1U boomless CubeSat technology** with **Quad-Mag technology** for noise reduction.

### ͧमशनको उद्देश्यहरू (Mission Objectives)

1. **To integrate Quad-Mag technology** for a boomless measurement system with noise reduction in 1U CubeSats.  
   १U क्यूबस्याटमा अनावश्यक तरङ्गहरू हटाई बूमलेस प्रविधिको प्रयोग गरी क्वाड-म्याग प्रविधिलाई एकीकृत गर्नु।

2. **To analyze electromagnetic wave signal patterns** to detect earthquake precursor signals.

3. **To demonstrate space-based technology** for detecting and predicting earthquakes from orbit.  
   अन्तरिक्षबाट भूकम्प पत्ता लगाउने र भविष्यवाणी गर्ने प्रविधिको प्रदर्शन गर्नु।

### Quad-Mag Technology and Signal Processing

The **Quad-Mag technology** is a **small, low-cost, and low-power magnetometer board** that combines **four PNI RM3100 sensors** with a microcontroller to detect and study the **Earth’s geomagnetic field** and **Ultra-Low Frequency (ULF) waves**.  

By integrating multiple sensors, the Quad-Mag system achieves **higher resolution and improved accuracy** compared to single fluxgate magnetometers. This makes it a **viable and scalable option** for geomagnetic studies both **in space and on the ground**.  

In space applications, the **EPDM payload** is designed to **operate efficiently in low-power environments** while maintaining **high sensitivity**, which is essential for advancing scientific missions that investigate **geomagnetic phenomena** and **space weather impacts**.  

To minimize noise and interference, the **Underdetermined Blind Source Separation (UBSS)** algorithm, developed by **Hoffmann and Moldwin**, is implemented in EPDM. This technique, **tested at the University of Michigan**, successfully **removes stray magnetic fields** originating from the satellite’s electrical systems, isolating only the **true ambient magnetic field**.  

By combining **Quad-Mag technology** with the **UBSS algorithm**, EPDM enables precise detection of **magnetic changes caused by earthquakes, solar storms, and other geomagnetic events**—making it a pioneering step toward **boomless magnetic sensing in CubeSats**.
